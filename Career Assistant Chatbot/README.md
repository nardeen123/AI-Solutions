💼 EVA Pharma Careers Assistant
Welcome to the EVA Pharma Careers Assistant — a smart conversational agent designed to help users explore job listings and 
understand opportunities at EVA Pharma. Whether you're a fresh graduate, a pharmacist, or an experienced professional, 
this assistant can guide you through available roles and support your career decisions with an intelligent and human-like experience.


-----------------------------------------------------------  🚀 Project Highlights   ------------------------------------------------------------------------
🗣️ Voice Input & Output
Users can speak to the assistant and also hear responses using Text-to-Speech (TTS), allowing a more natural and accessible experience.

🧠 Memory-Aware Dialogue
The assistant keeps track of the current session and context, enabling follow-up questions such as:
“Now show me pharmacist jobs in Cairo.”

📚 RAG-Inspired Architecture
Although not a full RAG model, the assistant mimics its behavior:
Retrieves matching questions from a FAQ dataset using fuzzy and semantic matching.
Responds with curated, human-written answers.

✅ Misspelling Tolerance
Handles common user spelling mistakes without losing accuracy or showing irrelevant results.

🎓 Personalized Suggestions
Understands whether the user is a fresh graduate, pharmacist, or experienced professional, and tailors job suggestions accordingly.

⚖️ Role Comparison
Allows users to compare two job roles side by side, helping them make informed career decisions.

📈 Career Path Summarization
Explains career progression at EVA Pharma, such as:
“What’s the typical path from Medical Rep to Area Manager?”

🔁 Real-Time Career Listings
The assistant connects to the live EVA Pharma careers website, providing up-to-date job opportunities with no manual updates required.

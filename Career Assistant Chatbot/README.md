# 💼 EVA Pharma Careers Assistant

Welcome to the **EVA Pharma Careers Assistant** — a smart conversational agent designed to help users explore job listings and understand opportunities at **EVA Pharma**.  

Whether you're a fresh graduate, a pharmacist, or an experienced professional, this assistant guides you through available roles and supports your career decisions with an intelligent and human-like experience.  

---

## 🚀 Project Highlights

### 🗣️ Voice Input & Output
- Users can speak to the assistant and hear responses using **Text-to-Speech (TTS)**, enabling a natural and accessible interaction.

### 🧠 Memory-Aware Dialogue
- Keeps track of the session and context for follow-up questions, e.g.:  
  *“Now show me pharmacist jobs in Cairo.”*

### 📚 RAG-Inspired Architecture
- Mimics **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** behavior:  
  - Retrieves matching questions from a **FAQ dataset** using fuzzy and semantic matching.  
  - Responds with curated, human-written answers.  

### ✅ Misspelling Tolerance
- Handles common user spelling mistakes without losing accuracy or providing irrelevant results.  

### 🎓 Personalized Suggestions
- Tailors job suggestions based on the user type: fresh graduate, pharmacist, or experienced professional.  

### ⚖️ Role Comparison
- Allows users to compare two job roles side by side for informed career decisions.  

### 📈 Career Path Summarization
- Explains typical career progression at EVA Pharma, e.g.:  
  *“What’s the typical path from Medical Rep to Area Manager?”*

### 🔁 Real-Time Career Listings
- Connects to the **live EVA Pharma careers website**, providing up-to-date job opportunities automatically.
